Bundled fonts - additional typefaces
======================================

This folder bundles the following fonts (in addition to Carlito,
see LICENSE-Carlito.txt) to give FrostSeek users a choice of bundled
typefaces that work on ANY client - including heavily modified custom
clients that do not expose any usable font to addons:

  * LiberationSans-Regular.ttf  (neutral sans, Arial metrics)
  * LiberationSerif-Regular.ttf (classic serif, Times metrics)
  * DejaVuSans.ttf              (sturdy, wide-coverage sans)
